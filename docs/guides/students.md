## 1. Signing in

Your parent or CLG sets up your login. Open the link in your welcome email,
set a password, then sign in at the CLG app.

> Screenshot to add: `screenshots/students-01-sign-in.png`, the sign-in page.

## 2. Your learning

Go to **Dashboard**. **Your learning** shows your goals, the skills you're
working on, and what to practise before your next session.

> Screenshot to add: `screenshots/students-02-learning.png`, Your learning.

## 3. Your sessions

Go to **Schedule**, then **Sessions** to see what's coming up, with your
tutor, the subject and where.

> Screenshot to add: `screenshots/students-03-sessions.png`, the Sessions list.

## 4. Joining an online session

Open the session under **Schedule** and choose **Join** (it opens Teams) a few
minutes before it starts.

> Screenshot to add: `screenshots/students-04-join.png`, a session with the Join link.

## 5. Study dates (university and TAFE)

1. Go to **Schedule**, then **Session places**.
2. Under **Study period**, choose **My own dates**.
3. Add each semester, trimester or term with its first and last day.

Next-term questions and holiday breaks follow these dates.

> Screenshot to add: `screenshots/students-05-study-dates.png`, adding own study dates.

## 6. Asking for an extra session

Go to **Schedule**, then **Requests**, then **Ask for a session**. Choose
the subject, the date of your test or exam, and any times that suit.

> Screenshot to add: `screenshots/students-06-request.png`, the request form.

## 7. Notices

Notices are in the app only, never between 9pm and 5am (your local time). See them under
**Schedule**, then **Notices**.
