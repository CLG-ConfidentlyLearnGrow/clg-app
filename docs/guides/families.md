## 1. Signing in

1. Open the link in your welcome email from CLG.
2. Set a password. Use at least 12 characters.
3. Next time, go to the CLG app and choose **Sign in**. If you forget your
   password, choose **Forgot password** and follow the email.

> Screenshot to add: `screenshots/families-01-sign-in.png`, the sign-in page.

Tip: you can add the app to your phone's home screen. Go to the app's
**Install** page and follow the steps for your phone.

## 2. Adding your child

1. Go to **Dashboard**, then **Add a child**.
2. Enter your child's name, date of birth, state, year level and school.
3. Choose the subjects you'd like help with.
4. Read and agree to the consents, then choose **Add child**.

> Screenshot to add: `screenshots/families-02-add-child.png`, the Add a child form.

## 3. Where, when and study dates

Go to **Schedule**, then **Session places**. For each child:

1. **Where**: tick **Online** and any libraries that suit. More places mean
   more tutors to choose from.
2. **Preferred days and parts of the day** (optional): tick days, and
   morning, middle of the day, afternoon or evening. These are
   preferences, not guarantees: they may not be met, depending on which
   tutors are free.
3. **Study period**: choose SA government school terms, SA private school
   terms (an extra week off between Terms 2 and 3), or your own dates for
   university or TAFE. For own dates, add each semester or term.
4. **Last day of Term 4**: enter your child's last day of school this year.
   It changes by school and year level (Year 12s often finish early).
5. Choose **Save** on each part.

> Screenshot to add: `screenshots/families-03-session-places.png`, Session places with the study period.

Sessions at home or at school are only for special circumstances and are
arranged by CLG. Please contact CLG to ask.

## 4. Booking a session

1. Go to **Schedule**, then **Book a session**.
2. Choose your child, the subject and how long (1 hour minimum).
3. The app shows times with tutors who can take it. Choose one.
4. The time is held for the tutor, who has 48 hours to confirm. You'll see
   it under **Bookings**, and get a notice when it's confirmed.
5. If no time suits, join the **waitlist**. Everyone on the waitlist is
   seen by CLG, longest wait first.

> Screenshot to add: `screenshots/families-04-book.png`, the list of available times.

## 5. Approving an offer from CLG

Sometimes CLG offers a time. Both you and the tutor need to approve it.

1. Go to **Schedule**, then **Bookings**.
2. Check the day, time, tutor and place, then choose **Approve** or
   **Decline**.
3. You have 3 days. After 2 days you'll get a reminder if you haven't
   answered. If you've approved and the tutor hasn't yet, you'll get a
   notice saying it's waiting on them.

> Screenshot to add: `screenshots/families-05-offer.png`, an offer waiting for approval.

## 6. Your sessions and cancelling

1. Go to **Schedule**, then **Sessions** to see what's coming up.
2. To cancel one session, choose **Cancel** next to it. Cancelling more
   than 24 hours before gives full credit; 2 to 24 hours before, half
   credit; under 2 hours, no credit.
3. To stop a booking (all future sessions), go to **Bookings** and choose
   **Stop booking**.

> Screenshot to add: `screenshots/families-06-sessions.png`, the Sessions list.

## 7. Away dates

1. Go to **Schedule**, then **Away dates**.
2. Choose your child, the first and last day away, and a reason if you like.
3. Choose **Save away dates**. Sessions on those days are cancelled.

If your **tutor** is away, you'll get a notice. It may include other times
your tutor can offer. You can ask for another time or a different tutor
(see the next step).

> Screenshot to add: `screenshots/families-07-away.png`, the Away dates form.

## 8. Asking for an extra or one-off session

1. Go to **Schedule**, then **Requests**, then **Ask for a session**.
2. Choose your child, the subject and what you'd like:
   - an extra session before a test, exam or other big event
   - a one-off at another time in a public holiday week
   - another time or tutor while your tutor is away
3. Add the date and any times that suit, then choose **Send request**.
4. Your tutor or CLG offers a time. Approve it under **Bookings**.

> Screenshot to add: `screenshots/families-08-request.png`, the Ask for a session form.

## 9. Public holidays

Under **Schedule**, then **Requests**, **Public holiday weeks** shows
sessions that fall on a public holiday. For each booking you can choose
**Skip public holidays**, then ask for a one-off at another time that week.

> Screenshot to add: `screenshots/families-09-public-holidays.png`, Public holiday weeks.

## 10. Next term

Three weeks before your child's study period ends, you'll get a notice.

1. Go to **Schedule**, then **Next term**.
2. For each booking, say whether you'd like the same again, and whether you
   want sessions in the holidays. If you'd like a change, say what and why.
3. You can also leave feedback, and say if CLG may use it as a testimonial.

> Screenshot to add: `screenshots/families-10-next-term.png`, the next-term question.

## 11. Notices

CLG sends notices in the app only, not by email. Nothing arrives between
9pm and 6am. Go to **Schedule**, then **Notices** to read them, and choose
when your first session reminder comes.

> Screenshot to add: `screenshots/families-11-notices.png`, the Notices list.

## 12. Progress and term reviews

1. From **Dashboard**, open your child's **Progress**.
2. See their goals, the skills they're building and session notes.
3. **Term reviews** are an optional paid extra, written by your child's
   tutor at the end of each term. Ask CLG to add them.

> Screenshot to add: `screenshots/families-12-progress.png`, the progress page.

## 13. Billing and payment plans

1. Go to **Billing**.
2. Read and agree to the payment terms before your first invoice.
3. Save a card or bank account on Stripe's secure page (CLG never sees
   your card details).
4. Payment plans (per year, term, month or week) are for ongoing tutoring
   only. One-off sessions and other services are invoiced separately.

> Screenshot to add: `screenshots/families-13-billing.png`, the Billing page.

## 14. If a tutor isn't the right fit

1. Go to **Schedule**, then **Blocks**, then **Block a tutor**.
2. Choose the tutor and write why (only CLG sees this).
3. The app stops suggesting that tutor for your child. The tutor isn't
   told. CLG will contact you to talk about what happened.

> Screenshot to add: `screenshots/families-14-block.png`, the Block a tutor form.

## 15. Your privacy

Go to **Your privacy** to download your information, ask CLG to fix or
delete it, or choose whether you get CLG news by email.

> Screenshot to add: `screenshots/families-15-privacy.png`, the Your privacy page.
