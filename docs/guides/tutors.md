## 1. Signing in and two-step sign in

1. Open your invitation link and set a password.
2. Set up two-step sign in with an authenticator app. You'll need it each
   time you sign in.

> Screenshot to add: `screenshots/tutors-01-two-step.png`, the two-step setup page.

## 2. Child safety checks

Go to **Dashboard**, then **Child safety checks**.

1. Add your working with children check for each state you'll tutor in.
2. Add your **RRHAN-EC** (Responding to Risks of Harm, Abuse and Neglect,
   Education and Care) certificate. It's required in every state.
3. For each, upload the certificate and enter the date it was done or the
   date it expires.
4. Optional: a police check and a first aid certificate.

CLG checks each one. You'll get a reminder 6 weeks and 2 weeks before a
check expires. You can't be booked in a state once a required check
expires.

> Screenshot to add: `screenshots/tutors-02-checks.png`, the Child safety checks section.

## 3. What you teach

Go to **Dashboard**, then **What you teach**. Choose your subjects and the
year levels for each.

> Screenshot to add: `screenshots/tutors-03-teaching.png`, What you teach.

## 4. Hours and availability

Go to **Dashboard**, then **Hours and availability**. Add the weekly times
you can tutor, and your maximum hours each week. Four weeks before each
term ends, you'll be asked to check these for next term.

> Screenshot to add: `screenshots/tutors-04-hours.png`, Hours and availability.

## 5. Where you teach

1. Go to **Schedule**, then **Where you teach**.
2. Choose **online**, **in person**, or both.
3. Tick the public libraries you can get to.
4. If a library isn't listed, add it under **Teach at a library that isn't
   listed?** CLG checks it and adds its opening hours before families can
   book it.

In-person sessions are only at public libraries CLG has confirmed, inside
opening hours. Sessions at a student's home or school are arranged by CLG
only.

> Screenshot to add: `screenshots/tutors-05-places.png`, Where you teach with the library note.

## 6. Confirming requests and offers

1. Go to **Schedule**, then **Bookings**.
2. A family's request: accept or decline within 48 hours.
3. An offer from CLG: you and the family both approve within 3 days. After
   2 days you'll get a reminder if you haven't answered.

> Screenshot to add: `screenshots/tutors-06-bookings.png`, a request waiting for you.

## 7. Your sessions: signing on and recording

1. Go to **Schedule**, then **Sessions**.
2. At a library, choose **Sign on** when you arrive (the app checks you're
   at the library).
3. After the session, record it as completed or a no-show, and confirm the
   end time.

> Screenshot to add: `screenshots/tutors-07-sign-on.png`, signing on to a session.

## 8. Away dates

1. Go to **Schedule**, then **Away dates**.
2. Enter the first and last day you're away.
3. Optional: add times you could offer instead. These are shared with the
   families.
4. Choose **Save away dates**.

Your sessions on those days are cancelled 2 hours after you save, so you
can remove the dates if you made a mistake. Families and CLG are then told
politely. Families can ask for another time or a different tutor.

> Screenshot to add: `screenshots/tutors-08-away.png`, the Away dates form with times you could offer.

## 9. Requests for extra or one-off sessions

1. Go to **Schedule**, then **Requests**.
2. For a request from one of your students' families, choose a date, start
   time, length and place, then **Offer this time**.
3. The family approves it under **Bookings**. An extra session must be
   before the event.

> Screenshot to add: `screenshots/tutors-09-requests.png`, offering a time for a request.

## 10. Progress notes and term reviews

1. From **Dashboard**, open a student's **Progress**.
2. After each session, add a short note on what you worked on and the next
   step.
3. **Term reviews** are only for students whose family has added them.
   You'll get a notice when one is due. Write it yourself. **Don't put any
   student information into ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini or any other AI
   tool.** CLG checks each review before the family sees it.

> Screenshot to add: `screenshots/tutors-10-term-review.png`, the term review form with the AI note.

## 11. Materials

Go to **Materials** for CLG's materials for your subjects and year levels.
Use them only in your CLG sessions.

## 12. My pay and admin hours

Go to **My pay** to see your pay for the last 8 weeks. If you do admin
work, log your hours by the day after each pay week ends. Late hours can
still be logged, but please be on time.

> Screenshot to add: `screenshots/tutors-12-pay.png`, My pay with the due date.

## 13. If a student isn't the right fit

1. Go to **Schedule**, then **Blocks**, then **Block a student**.
2. Choose the student and write why (only CLG sees this).
3. The app stops suggesting you together. The family isn't told. CLG will
   contact you to talk about what happened. Existing sessions aren't
   changed until then.

> Screenshot to add: `screenshots/tutors-13-block.png`, the Block a student form.

## 14. Notices

Notices are in the app only, never between 9pm and 5am (your local time). See them under
**Schedule**, then **Notices**. The **Sessions on hold or back on** card on
your dashboard shows families whose sessions are paused for billing.
