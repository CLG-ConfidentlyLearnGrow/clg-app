# CLG app guide for admins

Admin levels: **owner** and **operations** see everything; **front desk**
can book and help families; **finance** works in Billing. Some steps below
are only for some levels.

## 1. Signing in

Sign in with your password and two-step code. Admin pages are under
**Admin**.

> Screenshot to add: `screenshots/admins-01-admin-home.png`, the Admin page.

## 2. Families and matching

1. **Admin**, then **Families and matching**: set up a family, add children
   and logins.
2. **Schedule**, then **Matching**: subjects waiting for a tutor, oldest
   first. Choose **Book** to find a time.

> Screenshot to add: `screenshots/admins-02-matching.png`, the Matching list.

## 3. Tutors and checks

**Admin**, then **Tutors and checks**: verify each check against the
state's online register. RRHAN-EC is required in every state. You're told
2 weeks before a tutor's check expires.

> Screenshot to add: `screenshots/admins-03-checks.png`, checks waiting to verify.

## 4. Booking and offering times

1. **Schedule**, then **Book a session**. Choose the student, subject,
   length and place.
2. When you book for a family, it's an **offer**: the tutor and family each
   approve within 3 days. Whoever hasn't approved gets a reminder after
   2 days.
3. Quiet hours: nothing reaches anyone between 9pm and 5am, in their own
   local time; notices made then arrive at 5am. Owner and operations admins
   can change these times under **Schedule**, then **Notices**, then
   **Quiet hours (all of CLG)**.

> Screenshot to add: `screenshots/admins-04-book.png`, booking for a family.

## 5. Home or school sessions

Only CLG can book a session at a student's home or school (special
circumstances). Choose **At home** or **At school** when booking or
offering a one-off.

## 6. Requests and public holidays

**Schedule**, then **Requests**: families' requests for an extra session
before an event, a public holiday one-off, or cover while the tutor is
away. Offer a time with any tutor, or choose **Couldn't arrange it** with a
note. When a tutor saves away dates, you're told after 2 hours which
sessions were cancelled.

> Screenshot to add: `screenshots/admins-06-requests.png`, the Requests list.

## 7. Waitlist

**Schedule**, then **Waitlist**: everyone waiting, longest wait first.

## 8. Next term and study dates

1. **Schedule**, then **Next term**: families' answers about next term
   (asked 3 weeks before each student's study period ends).
2. **Study dates to check**: tertiary students with no study dates, and
   school students whose last day of Term 4 isn't confirmed. Ask the family
   and add it under **Session places**.
3. **School terms**: add or correct government and private school terms.
   Private schools have the same dates except Term 2 ends a week earlier.

> Screenshot to add: `screenshots/admins-08-study-dates.png`, Study dates to check.

## 9. Libraries and opening hours

1. **Schedule**, then **Libraries**.
2. Libraries marked **to confirm** were suggested by tutors. Check the
   library, add its opening hours (tick the days, then opens and closes),
   then choose **Confirm library**.
3. Sessions are only offered at confirmed libraries, inside opening hours.

> Screenshot to add: `screenshots/admins-09-libraries.png`, a library with opening hours.

## 10. Blocks

**Schedule**, then **Blocks**. Owner and operations admins are told when a
tutor or family blocks a match.

1. Contact the person who made the block. The other person isn't told.
2. Record what was discussed and what will help next time, then **Save
   follow-up**.
3. **Lift this block** only if the match should be suggested again.

> Screenshot to add: `screenshots/admins-10-blocks.png`, a block waiting for follow-up.

## 11. Term reviews and add-ons

**Admin**, then **Progress**:

1. **Add-ons**: term reviews are an opt-in paid add-on. The owner sets the
   price and switches it to paid; admins add it for each student who pays.
2. Check each tutor's term review and approve it, or send it back.

> Screenshot to add: `screenshots/admins-11-add-ons.png`, the Add-ons card.

## 12. Billing, prices and pay

**Admin**, then **Billing** (finance, owner and operations):

1. **Prices**: sessions $95 (school) and $115 (tertiary). Drafting $90
   (school) and $110 (tertiary), GST-free. Add drafting to an invoice by
   hand.
2. Payment plans (per year, term, month or week) are for ongoing tutoring
   only.
3. **Pay**: approve admin hours. Hours logged after the due date (the day
   after the pay week ends) are marked **logged late**.

> Screenshot to add: `screenshots/admins-12-billing.png`, the Prices card.

## 13. Recording why someone left

1. **Admin**, then **Leaving**, then **Record someone leaving**.
2. Choose the family or tutor, the day they left and the main reason.
3. For a family who left happy, ask for a testimonial or Google review and
   tick that you asked.

> Screenshot to add: `screenshots/admins-13-leaving.png`, the Leaving section.

## 14. Staff, emergency access and privacy

- **Admin**, then **Staff and accounts**: invite staff and set admin
  levels.
- **Emergency owner access**: if the owner is unreachable, an operations
  admin can ask for owner access. It starts after 24 hours unless the owner
  approves it sooner or cancels it. The owner gets an email.
- **Admin**, then **Privacy**: privacy requests (reply within 28 days).

## 15. Reports (owner only)

Reports show how long students wait for a tutor, how many new students book,
how full each tutor's week is, why people left, and profit per tutoring hour.
Only the owner sees them, because they include pay and profit.

- **Reports** in the admin menu. A number shows when a new report is ready.
- The app makes a **monthly** report on the 1st, a **quarterly** report after
  each quarter, and a **financial year** report each July.
- **Report for your dates**: choose a first and last day, or tap a common
  range such as **Last month**. The report is saved so you can open it again.
- **Download spreadsheet (CSV)** on any report opens in Excel or Google
  Sheets.
- **Settings**: super on wages (12%) and other running costs a month (rent,
  software, insurance and so on), used for "profit after all costs".
- If a report says some sessions have no pay worked out or aren't invoiced
  yet, the profit figures will change once billing catches up. Make a new
  report for the same dates to see the updated figures.
