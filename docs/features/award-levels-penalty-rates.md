# Award levels and penalty rates for school tutoring

Requested by Carla, 18 Nov 2026 ("Yes, please check that"), after the tutor
contract review found two Miscellaneous Award rules the app did not apply:
the move from Level 1 to Level 2 after 3 months, and penalty rates for
evenings, early mornings, weekends and public holidays.

## 1. What it is for

1. **Level 2 at 3 months.** The app checked every school tutoring rate
   against the Level 1 minimum. Under the award, Level 1 lasts only the first
   3 months. After that the minimum is Level 2 ($33.85 casual, $27.08
   part-time or full-time, from 1 July 2026). A tutor on a rate between those
   two figures would be underpaid from the 3-month mark.
2. **Penalty rates.** Much tutoring happens after school, after 7pm and on
   weekends. The award sets higher minimum rates for that time. CLG's casual
   contract already promises: "Where an award sets a higher rate for the time
   you work, such as weekday evenings after 7pm, mornings before 7am,
   weekends or public holidays, CLG pays that higher rate." The app now
   works out and pays that difference when a tutor's own rate is lower.

## 2. What the evidence says

- **Tutor quality and continuity drive results.** The Education Endowment
  Foundation rates one-to-one and small group tuition among the highest
  impact approaches (about +5 months' progress), with the strongest effects
  when tutoring is consistent and delivered by well-supported tutors. The
  independent evaluations of England's National Tutoring Programme found
  that recruiting and keeping tutors was one of the main limits on how much
  tuition reached students.
- **Fair, correct pay supports retention.** AERO's work on the teaching
  workforce, and the wider Australian workforce research it draws on,
  links pay, workload and feeling valued with whether educators stay.
  Paying the correct award rate every time, and showing tutors clearly why
  they received extra pay, supports the stable tutor-learner matches the
  research favours.
- **Clear records build trust.** Each top-up line explains the minutes,
  the time band and the award rate, so a tutor can check it themselves.

## 3. How it works

### Levels (Miscellaneous Award 2020, MA000104, cl 12.1)

- Level 1: employed for less than 3 months. Level 2: employed for at least
  3 months. Level 3: trade qualification or equivalent, used in the job.
  Level 4: advanced trade qualification, or a sub-professional employee.
- Each staff member's pay details now hold **Start date with CLG** and a
  **School tutoring award level**: "Level 1 or 2 (worked out from the start
  date)", Level 3 or Level 4.
- The daily billing run raises any school rate below the minimum for the
  person's level. At the 3-month mark the raise starts from that day (work
  before it stays at the old rate), the owner and Finance admins get an app
  notice once, and the Xero to-do list says to change the classification.
- **Start dates needed** on Staff pay lists tutors with a school rate but no
  start date. Until a start date is saved the app treats them as Level 1.
- Award rates are now kept by level. The 2026-27 school Levels 2 to 4 are
  loaded from the Fair Work pay guide. The yearly upload file has a level
  column; older files without it are read as Level 1.

### Penalty rates (cl 20, with cl 11.1 casual loading)

| Time worked (Adelaide time) | Full/part-time | Casual |
|---|---|---|
| Monday to Friday, 7am to 7pm | 100% | 125% |
| Monday to Friday, before 7am or after 7pm | 120% | 145% |
| Saturday | 120% | 145% |
| Sunday | 150% | 175% |
| Public holiday | 250% | 250% |

Percentages are of the base (full-time) hourly rate for the person's level.
The casual figures already include the 25% casual loading. The 2026-27 pay
guide gives the same dollar figures (for example Level 2 casual: $39.27
Saturday, $47.39 Sunday, $67.70 public holiday).

- When pay is worked out, each school tutoring session's paid time is split
  into these bands, minute by minute. Public holidays come from the app's
  public holidays list for the state the tutor is based in (their work
  settings; SA if not set). Carla, 18 Nov 2026: tutors are only paid public
  holiday rates when it is a public holiday in the state they are based in.
- For each band, if the tutor's own hourly rate is below the award rate,
  the difference is paid on a **Penalty rate top-up** line. There is no
  averaging across bands, so a high weekday rate never hides a shortfall on
  a Sunday.
- Top-up lines carry no hours (the session line already counts them), so
  hours totals, the casual minimum and agreed-hours checks are unchanged.
- Xero pays timesheet hours at each person's earnings rate, so top-ups
  cannot go as hours. When timesheets are sent, each person's top-ups become
  one Xero to-do with the dollar amount to add to that pay run. Nothing in
  Xero changes by itself, matching the earlier decision for rate changes.

### Not covered yet

- Tertiary tutoring (Post-Secondary Award MA000075) and office admin
  (Clerks Award MA000002) penalty rates. Both awards have their own rules
  and need their own check.
- The 15 minutes of summary and planning time after a session. The tutor
  chooses when to do it, so it is paid at the ordinary rate.
- Overtime (more than 10 hours in a day or an average of 38 a week, cl 19),
  which is unlikely for CLG's casual tutors today.
- The casual 2-hour minimum engagement (cl 11.2) is still switched off in
  the app by Carla's earlier choice; the Pay page keeps its warning.

## 4. Legal notes

- **Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth)**: s 45 (an employer must not contravene a term
  of an award), s 323 (pay amounts in full), s 535 and Fair Work Regulations
  reg 3.33 (records of the classification and of penalty rates paid), and
  reg 3.46 (payslips must show any penalty rate or loading amount
  separately; the Xero pay run line does this).
- **Wage underpayment offence**: since 1 January 2025, intentionally
  underpaying wages is a criminal offence (s 327A). The Voluntary Small
  Business Wage Compliance Code protects small businesses that make honest
  mistakes and fix them, which is why the app raises rates and flags
  shortfalls automatically.
- **Public holidays**: under the National Employment Standards (s 114) an
  employer may ask an employee to work on a public holiday if the request is
  reasonable, and the employee may refuse on reasonable grounds. CLG's
  family terms already say there are no sessions on SA public holidays
  unless agreed.
- **Privacy**: start dates and levels are employee records (exempt from the
  APPs for current and former employees under Privacy Act s 7B(3)), but they
  are still visible only to the owner and Finance admins.
- **UK and EU, for later**: UK National Minimum Wage rules have no penalty
  rates but strict record keeping; EU working time rules cover rest and
  night work. Both would need their own settings before CLG employs staff
  there.
- The award text was read from awards.fairwork.gov.au (amendments to
  27 August 2024) and the rates from the 2026-27 Fair Work pay guide. Check
  each year's pay guide when uploading new rates. Lawyer review of the
  contracts and pay set-up is still planned.
