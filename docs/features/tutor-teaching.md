# What each tutor teaches

## What it does
- When a tutor first signs in, they are asked for each subject they tutor and the year levels for that subject. For example, Maths from Year 3 to Year 10 and English from Year 7 to Year 12. They can change this later under "What you teach".
- Matching only lists a tutor for a student if:
  - the tutor is cleared for the student's state, and
  - the tutor teaches at least one subject at the student's year level.
- The admin chooses a tutor and one of the subjects that tutor teaches.
- The database also refuses any assignment where the tutor doesn't teach that subject at that year level, even if the request comes from an admin. A past assignment (with an end date already gone) can still be recorded.

## Research basis
- Tutor subject knowledge matters most where content is harder. EEF's tutoring guidance stresses matching tutors to the content and age being taught. AERO's guidance on effective tutoring says the same: tutoring works best when the tutor knows the curriculum and pedagogy for the year level.
  - EEF: "Making a Difference with Effective Tutoring" (2021).
  - AERO: "Tutoring: evidence and practice" (2023).
- Teaching the early years well is different from teaching senior years, so each subject has its own year-level range, not one range for the tutor.

## Legal rules touched
- None new. This sits beside the existing check rules: a tutor still needs every verified, in-date check for the student's state (WWCC, RRHAN-EC and a police check within 5 years). This feature adds a second condition and doesn't weaken the first.
- Tutors' subject lists are visible only to the tutor and to admins (row level security).
