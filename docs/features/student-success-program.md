## What it is for

Families in the Student Success Program pay a $500 deposit to secure their child's place for a planned run of sessions. The program bundles the parts of CLG that the evidence says matter most for steady progress: a written plan, regular reviews against it, and a consistent tutor found quickly. Places are limited, because CLG can only promise priority matching to as many students as its tutors can take.

## What the evidence says

- **One to one tuition** (EEF Teaching and Learning Toolkit, about +5 months): works best when sessions are regular, sustained over a planned period, and linked to what the student is learning. A planned number of sessions and a book-until date make the run regular and sustained.
- **Feedback** (EEF, about +6 months) and **metacognition and self-regulation** (EEF, about +7 months): students gain most when they know where they are going, how they are going, and what to do next. The study plan sets the goals; the term review (included at no charge) checks progress against them each term.
- **Parental engagement** (EEF, about +4 months): personalised updates about a child's progress work better than general ones. The term review is written by the student's own tutor and checked by CLG. Asking the family to opt in (rather than CLG adding the child) also makes the family an active partner from the start.
- **Tutor consistency and dosage** (Evidence for Learning and AERO guidance on high-impact tutoring, drawing on the Stanford National Student Support Accelerator): a stable relationship with one tutor, enough sessions, and quick matching reduce lost learning time. Program students go first in Matching. Limiting places protects that promise: priority means little if every student has it.

## How the design follows it

1. **The family asks.** Under each child on the family dashboard, **Student Success Program** explains the program and has a tick box ("Yes, I would like to ask about a place") with an optional message. An adult learner who manages their own account asks from their own dashboard. The family can cancel the request until CLG answers.
2. **An admin decides.** Owner and Operations admins see a **Student Success Program** card in Families: places used and left, requests waiting, places waiting for the deposit (with the forfeit time), places with the deposit paid and the rest being paid, and students in the program. Declining sends the family an optional reason. The database checks the number of places inside a lock, so two admins can never give away the last place twice.
3. **Approving makes the program invoices.** In Change a student's details the admin enters the program total (deposit included), the payment plan (pay in full, monthly or weekly) and the finish payment date, plus the sessions planned, book-until date and a note. Billing then makes, straight away and already approved:
   - payment 1, the $500 deposit, due within 72 hours and charged from the saved card or bank account on the next daily billing run;
   - then one payment of the rest on the finish date (pay in full), or equal payments one month or one week apart until the finish date (any odd cents go on the last one), each with a plan fee line ($10 monthly, $5 weekly; both are editable prices in Billing).
   The admin sees a plain summary, for example "$500.00 deposit now, then 3 monthly payments of about $500.00 plus a $10.00 plan fee each, the last on 22 February 2027. Total $2,030.00." Every invoice goes to Xero as an approved invoice on the next billing run.
4. **Reminders and deadlines** (an hourly job): at 48 hours the payer is reminded that the place will be forfeited if the deposit is not paid; at 72 hours an unpaid deposit forfeits the place. Before each later payment the payer is reminded 24 hours ahead. A missed payment brings a warning straight away and a final warning 2 days before the place is forfeited; the place is forfeited a set number of days after the missed due date (7 to start, a setting on the card), unless the admin has recorded a payment agreement that is still running. For a family paying weekly, sessions credited because the weekly invoice was not paid, two weeks in a row (with no plan agreed with CLG), also forfeit the place (Carla, 23 Nov 2026). Forfeiting cancels the unpaid program invoices, ends anything the program gave, puts the student back on the waiting list (with the reason kept), tells the family, and tells the owner and Operations admins that a place is open, why, and which family it was for. Payment details go only to the people who pay (billing information is for payers only).
5. **Deposit paid means secured; everything paid means in.** When billing marks the deposit paid (Stripe, Pay now, or a payment entered in Xero) the place is secured and the family is told. When every program invoice is paid, the student is in the program straight away: the study plan add-on is given, term reviews are included, the violet label shows in Matching and they go first in the queue. The family is told.
6. **Changes.** Admins can change a place's details, record or end a payment agreement, withdraw a place before the deposit is paid (the invoices are cancelled and the place freed), or remove a student whose deposit is paid (unpaid invoices are cancelled and the program study plan ends; any refund follows CLG's terms and is done in Billing). If a deposit invoice is cancelled in Xero before it is paid, the place is freed, the rest of the program invoices are cancelled, and the request goes back to the waiting list.
7. **Never a session hold.** Unpaid program invoices never put the family's sessions on hold and never lock the family's session payment plan. A missed program payment leads to forfeiting the place instead.

## How we will know it works

- requests per term, and how many become paid places
- days from request to decision, and from approval to deposit paid
- places forfeited (deposit and later payments) and payment agreements recorded
- days from deposit to first session for program students, against everyone else
- share of program students with a term review sent each term
- share of program students who finish their planned sessions, and who re-enrol

## Privacy, child safety and legal notes

- **Privacy Act 1988 (APPs 3, 6 and 11) and the Children's Online Privacy Code**: the program record holds only what CLG needs (status, dates, sessions planned, an optional family message and an admin note). Families read their own child's status through a function that leaves out CLG's own note; only front desk admins read the whole list, and only owner and Operations admins change it. Tutors do not see requests. The note boxes are not for health or diagnosis details. Every change is in the audit log, and the record is deleted with the student. Deposit invoices are kept for 7 years like all invoices.
- **Australian Consumer Law**: CLG's family terms must say what the deposit is for (it is the first payment of the program total), what the plan fees are, when payments are taken automatically, when a place is forfeited after a missed payment, and when anything is refunded. Forfeiting a place after a missed payment should come with a clear warning and a fair chance to pay or agree a payment plan, which the reminders and payment agreements provide. A deposit that is not refundable in any situation, or is much larger than CLG's real costs of holding a place, risks being an unfair contract term (ACL sections 23 to 25, with penalties since November 2023). The consumer guarantees still apply. The app records and collects the deposit but does not refund it; refunds follow the terms and are done in Billing.
- **Consent to charges**: ticking the box is a request for information, not an agreement to pay. Payments are taken from the saved card or bank account the payer set up under the family terms (Stripe holds the mandate), and the payer is told about the offer and reminded 24 hours before each later payment. The family terms need wording that covers program payments, the plan fees and forfeiting (to add with Carla's lawyer review).
- **GST**: Carla treats curriculum tutoring as GST-free, and the deposit and payments are towards that program, so the price items (deposit, program and plan fees) start GST-free. The setting can be changed on the price item. Detailed GST questions belong in Carla's finance work with her accountant.
- **UK and EU (GDPR)**: the same data minimisation, access and retention rules apply if CLG expands. A deposit would also need to meet UK consumer contract rules on cancellation.
